## 1. What this chapter is really about

The village of Wenmere sank forty years ago and nobody talks about why. The players should leave with **one answer and two better questions**. Keep the tone quiet and sad rather than scary; the danger is the river, not the ghosts.

Three things have to come out of it:

1. **Old Mara knew.** She ferried the last villagers out and kept a promise to stay silent.
2. **The bell still rings** at moonrise, and it rings for someone in the party.
3. **The river can be bargained with**, at a price the party will meet again in Chapter 4.

## 2. The road and the ferry

> [!readaloud] The ferry landing
> The road ends in reeds. A flat-bottomed ferry waits at a sagging jetty, and an old woman sits on its rail mending a net that has more holes than knots. She does not look up.
> «Πέντε χάλκινα το κεφάλι. Τα άλογα πληρώνουν διπλά, γιατί φοβούνται το νερό.»

- **Old Mara** wants to get the crossing over with. She fears the far bank after dark. Tell: she counts the passengers twice.
- If a character asks about Wenmere: [[/check skill=ins dc=13]] notices her hands stop moving.
- Persuading her to talk: [[/check skill=per dc=15]]. On a failure she raises the price instead.

> [!whisper] Ilsa
> You have heard this voice before. It sang you to sleep once, in a house you no longer remember.

> [!dm] If they skip the ferry
> Swimming the river: [[/save ability=con dc=12]] per round, three rounds. A failure means [[/damage 1d6 cold]] and the current carries them a hundred feet downstream, onto the village's drowned roofs.

## 3. The drowned village

The water has drawn back for the summer. Roofs, a well, and a bell tower stand out of the mud.

### 3a. The well

- Inside: a rope that goes down further than the well is deep.
- Pulling it up brings a bucket full of dry, warm sand. **[HB]**
- [[monster:Swarm of Insects]] (use the 2024 stat block) nest in the bucket's rim.

### 3b. The bell tower

> [!readaloud] Moonrise
> The bell has no rope and no clapper, yet it hums. As the moon clears the hills, the hum becomes a single clear note, and every door in the village swings open at once.

> [!secret] The bell rings for Ilsa
> The note matches the lullaby from the ferry. Whoever carries the letter from Session 00 hears their own name in it.

| d6 | What comes through the doors |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Nothing; only cold air smelling of river weed |
| 3–4 | Two [[monster:Specter]] that only follow, never attack first |
| 5 | A child's voice asking for the ferry |
| 6 | [[Captain Ilvane]], dripping, who thinks it is forty years ago |

### 3c. Captain Ilvane

- **Wants:** to finish evacuating the village. **Fears:** being told it is over. **Tell:** salutes before every sentence.
- He carries a ledger of names; the last line is unfinished. See [[#4. The river spirit]].

## 4. The river spirit

> [!readaloud] The water parts
> The river draws back like a curtain. Where the deepest pool should be, a figure of moving water kneels, holding the missing clapper of the bell in both hands.

Three riddles, one per character who answers. A wrong answer costs a memory (the player describes a small one their character loses). %%Keep the riddles short; the table is tired by now.%%

- Riddle answers: *a promise*, *an echo*, *a name*.
- If they attack: it uses [[spell:Ice Storm]] once, then sinks and the chapter ends badly but goes on.
- If they bargain: it returns the clapper for one future favour. Record it in the [[Payoff Ledger]].

## 5. Rewards

- 150 XP each for the bargain, or 100 for driving the spirit off.
- The bell clapper (`DMG, Ch. 7 › Magic Items` style trinket **[HB]**): once per day, ring it to learn whether a body of water is safe to cross.
- Old Mara's silence is broken; she becomes a source in Chapter 2.

## 6. If they go off-script

- **They leave the village before moonrise:** the bell rings anyway; they hear it from the road and Ilsa's player gets the whisper.
- **They burn the ledger:** Ilvane fades with a relieved smile; the spirit refuses to bargain.
- **They bring Old Mara along:** she stays on the ferry and sings, which keeps the specters calm for one round each.
